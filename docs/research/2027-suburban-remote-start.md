## Model-specific answer

Each start lasts up to **15 minutes**, within a **30-minute cumulative budget**. That permits two full runs or more shorter runs. Starting the vehicle normally and then switching it off restores remote-start availability. The fob uses two presses of the remote-start button. These are the “Remote Start” and “Total Engine Run Time” sections, printed pages **14–15** (PDF pages **15–16**). [Official 2027 manual](https://contentdelivery.ext.gm.com/content/dam/cope/en_us/public/pdf_assets/active/owners_manuals_browse/2027/chevrolet/tahoe-suburban/27_CHEV_Tahoe_Suburban_OM_en_US_U_85836475A_2026JUL15.pdf#page=15)

Conditions include ignition off, Park, closed hood, fob outside, hazards off, and no emission-system malfunction indication. The section does not specify a separate extension gesture. Chevrolet's current app guide uses the Start/Climate control; on equipped vehicles, another tap cancels the command.
